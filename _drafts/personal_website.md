I’ve finally decided to create a proper personal website to showcase my research, projects, and interests. To be honest, this was a daunting step for me; I’ve always been a bit hesitant to share my work-in-progress with the world. 

However, I’ve come to realize how vital visibility is in the research community. The process of building my project pages was surprisingly rewarding, sparking a newfound appreciation for open discourse. I realized that if I want to advocate for transparency and dialogue in science, I have to be an active participant in it. 

Beyond the technical work, I’m also excited to use this blog to share my hobbies. I truly believe that by sharing our full selves—both professional and personal—we can lead more authentic and joyful lives. Welcome to my corner of the web!
