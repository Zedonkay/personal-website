For the first time ever, I've decided to create a proper personal website to showcase my research, projects, and interests.

I'm using the [al-folio](https://github.com/alshedivat/al-folio) Jekyll theme, which is designed specifically for academics and researchers. It has great features for displaying publications, projects, blog posts, and more.

## Why a personal website?

Having a personal website helps me:

- **Share my research** with the broader community
- **Showcase projects** I've worked on
- **Connect with others** in robotics and AI
- **Document my journey** through blog posts

## What's next?

I'm planning to add more content, including:

- Detailed project pages
- Research updates
- Technical blog posts about robotics and machine learning
- Interesting findings from my work

Stay tuned for more updates!
