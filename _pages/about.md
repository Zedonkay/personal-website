Hi, I'm **Ishayu Shikhare**!

I'm a student at Carnegie Mellon University passionate about robotics, machine learning, and bio-inspired systems. My research interests include developing robust locomotion controllers for legged robots and exploring emergent behaviors in distributed neural systems.

Currently, I'm working on projects related to robot locomotion, neural networks, and computational models inspired by biological systems. I'm particularly interested in how we can create more adaptive and resilient robotic systems by drawing inspiration from nature.

When I'm not working on robotics and research, you can find me watching films (check out my [Letterboxd](https://letterboxd.com/Zedonkay/)) or exploring new technologies.

Feel free to reach out via [email](mailto:ishikhar@andrew.cmu.edu) to discuss research, collaboration opportunities, or just to chat about robotics and AI!
