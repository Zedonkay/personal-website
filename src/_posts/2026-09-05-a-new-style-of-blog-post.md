I recently sought to bring life to this blog. Unfortunately, I found that I too easily fell into the trap of over editing, to the point that my posts lost my voice.

And so, I have made the radical decision to write my posts by hand. I recently got a new fountain pen, and I have found writing cursive with it on nice paper to have brought back an enjoyment of writing in me that I had long since thought forgotten.

Enjoy these unadulterated windows into my mind.
